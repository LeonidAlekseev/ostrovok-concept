Photos were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons; all images are processed by
`scripts/build-images.py` (crop, resize, WebP; the globe is rendered from the
equirectangular texture with an orthographic projection; `earth-texture-*` is the
cropped texture itself, rendered live by the WebGL globe).
